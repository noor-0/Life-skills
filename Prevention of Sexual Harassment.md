# Prevention of Sexual Harassment


**1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**
Any unwanted behavior of a sexual nature, such as inappropriate comments, jokes, messages, touching, staring, repeated advances, or sharing offensive content that makes someone feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected.

**2. What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?**
I would stay calm, support the affected person if needed, document the incident, and report it through the appropriate channel (manager, HR, or the Internal Committee). I believe such behavior should never be ignored.
