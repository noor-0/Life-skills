# Listening and Active Communication

### Q1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

**Ans.** Steps/Strategies for Active Listening are:

* Avoid getting distracted by my own thoughts and focus entirely on the speaker and the topic.
* Let the other person finish talking completely before I respond.
* When appropriate, write down notes during important conversations.
* Repeat what the other person said in your own words to ensure that both of you are on the same page.

---

### Q2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

* Try to see the world from the speaker's point of view.
* Do not judge, criticize, or even express agreement or disagreement.
* Encourage the speaker to talk about real, specific events.
* Pay close attention to their tone of voice, body posture, and feelings—not just their literal words.
* Do not ask nosy questions, share your own beliefs, or try to solve their problem.

---

### Q3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

**Ans.** I have a hard time concentrating. My brain wanders off—it takes a chunk of words and starts processing them or finding good jokes in them.

I also sometimes can't stop judging people. (Sometimes that makes me take them the wrong way.)

---

### Q4. What can you do to improve your listening?

**Ans.** Try to refocus whenever I lose focus, and try not to show judgment or disagreement.

---

### Q5. When do you switch to a Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

**Ans.** When I am talking to people working for me, like waiters, sweepers, or others.

---

### Q6. When do you switch into an Aggressive communication style in your day-to-day life?

**Ans.** When the other person is forgetting their own limits and boundaries and tries to interfere with other people's boundaries.

---

### Q7. When do you switch into a Passive-Aggressive communication style (sarcasm, gossiping, taunts, silent treatment, etc.) in your day-to-day life?

**Ans.** With some idiot friends/people, just to casually remind them of their boundaries.

---

### Q8. How can you make your communication assertive?

* Understanding and clearly stating my needs.
* Being aware of my body language and tone; otherwise, I can sound aggressive.
* Addressing or pointing out behaviors as early as possible.
