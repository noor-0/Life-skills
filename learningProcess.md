# Learning Process

### Q1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

**Ans.** If you want to learn something deeply, **explain it to someone else.**

### Q3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Ans.** Active mode is when you are the pilot of your own thoughts. Diffuse mode is like in Iron Man when you call 'Jarvis' to take over—it's basically autopilot where your brain is free to wander by itself.

---

### Q4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

**Ans.** The steps are:

* Deconstruct/Break down the skill
* Learn enough to self-correct
* Remove barriers to practice
* Pre-commit to the time
